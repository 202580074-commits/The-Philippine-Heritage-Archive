What is the Archive?

The Philippine Heritage Archive is a collaborative, non-commercial project documenting the country's history, languages, traditions, and natural heritage. Every entry is written to be readable by anyone, no academic background required.

Our goal is to fill the gap left by general-purpose encyclopedias, which often cover the Philippines only in passing. Here, it is the main subject.

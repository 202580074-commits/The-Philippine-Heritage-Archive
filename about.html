About the Archive

The Philippine Heritage Archive is a free, open reference project documenting the history, culture, languages, and natural heritage of the Philippines. It is written for students, researchers, and anyone curious about the country's past and present.

Why this exists

General-purpose encyclopedias cover the Philippines only in passing. Topics that matter deeply here — regional languages, pre-colonial societies, local architecture, oral epics — often have short or missing entries elsewhere. This archive treats them as the main subject.

How it works

Every entry is written to be readable without an academic background, but grounded in sources that are cited and checkable. Where a topic is contested, we say so rather than pick a side.

Who it's for

Students writing papers, teachers preparing lessons, researchers looking for a starting point, and anyone who has ever wondered what a place was called before it was renamed.
